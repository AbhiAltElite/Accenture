# Product Supply and Distribution Planning — Standard Operating Procedure

Version 3.1 · Supply Chain Directorate · Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose

This procedure governs the monthly product supply and distribution cycle for
refined petroleum products across all four marketing regions. It defines the
inputs to the cycle, the sequence in which they are reconciled, and the
approvals required before an allocation plan is released to terminals.

## 2. Cycle cadence

The cycle runs monthly with a weekly rolling revision. The monthly plan is
locked on the twenty-second of the preceding month. Weekly revisions are
permitted for allocation only and may not reopen the pricing assumptions.

## 3. Inputs to the planning cycle

The following inputs are consolidated by the supply desk before the plan is
built. No plan may be released without all four.

Historical offtake by region, product family and offtake route, drawn from the
terminal dispatch records for the preceding twelve months and seasonally
adjusted against the equivalent period of the prior year.

Terminal and pipeline inventory position as at the lock date, including product
held in transit and product committed against confirmed indents but not yet
lifted.

Refinery production programme and evacuation capacity for the plan period,
including any planned turnaround windows notified by the refineries and the
resulting derated throughput.

Financial plan and margin targets by product family, including the import
parity assumptions the pricing committee has approved for the period.

## 4. Reconciliation sequence

Step one reconciles historical offtake against the production programme to
establish an unconstrained requirement. Step two applies inventory position to
derive a net requirement. Step three applies evacuation capacity to derive the
allocable quantity. Step four tests the allocable quantity against the financial
plan and escalates any shortfall to the directorate.

## 5. Approvals

The regional supply managers approve their own allocation. The directorate
approves the consolidated plan. No terminal may lift against an unapproved
allocation.

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions are handled through the daily supply call. An exception raised on the
daily call does not amend the plan; it authorises a departure from it, which is
reconciled at the next weekly revision.
