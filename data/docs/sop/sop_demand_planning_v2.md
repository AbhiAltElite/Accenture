# Regional Demand Planning — Standard Operating Procedure

Version 2.1 · Owner: Supply Planning · Review cycle: annual

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the monthly demand planning cycle for regional categories
and the inputs that cycle consumes. It exists so that the plan is reproducible
between planners and auditable after the fact.

## 2. Cycle inputs

The planner assembles the following before the consensus meeting.

Historical sales are extracted for the trailing twenty-four months at category and region grain.
Inventory position is taken from the warehouse system as at the close of the preceding week.
Capacity metrics are provided by operations for each distribution centre.
The financial plan is supplied by FP&A as the current approved budget.

## 3. Cycle steps

1. **Statistical baseline.** Generate the baseline forecast from historical sales.
2. **Commercial overlay.** Category managers adjust for planned promotions and
   assortment changes.
3. **Supply reconciliation.** Compare the adjusted plan against inventory
   position and capacity metrics; flag any category where the plan exceeds
   available supply.
4. **Financial reconciliation.** Compare the reconciled plan against the
   financial plan and escalate material gaps.
5. **Consensus meeting.** Agree a single plan of record.

## 4. Escalation

Where the reconciled plan differs from the financial plan by more than five per
cent at region level, the category manager escalates to the commercial director
before the plan is published.

## 5. Out of scope

Intra-month replanning, customer-specific allocations, and new product
introduction planning are covered by separate procedures.
