# Generation Scheduling and Despatch — Standard Operating Procedure

Version 2.4 · System Operations · Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose

This procedure governs the day-ahead and intra-day scheduling of the generating
fleet across all four grid regions. It defines the inputs to the scheduling
cycle, the order in which they are applied, and the approvals required before a
schedule is declared to the load despatch centre.

## 2. Cycle cadence

Day-ahead schedules are declared by 1800 hours for the following day. Intra-day
revisions are permitted at each block boundary and must be notified to the load
despatch centre before the block begins.

## 3. Inputs to the scheduling cycle

The following inputs are consolidated by the scheduling desk before a schedule
is declared. No schedule may be declared without all four.

Historical generation and offtake by station, fuel and contracting route, drawn
from the metered energy accounts for the preceding twelve months and adjusted
for the equivalent period of the prior year.

Fuel stock position at each station as at the declaration cut-off, including
coal in the yard, coal in transit against the rake programme, and contracted gas
availability for the period.

Declared capacity and planned outage programme for the fleet, including any
derating notified by the stations and the transmission corridor limits published
by the transmission licensee.

Financial plan and variable cost targets by station, including the merit order
position each station is expected to hold against the approved cost stack.

## 4. Application sequence

Step one establishes the unconstrained availability from declared capacity. Step
two applies fuel stock position to derive the sustainable availability. Step
three applies corridor limits to derive the schedulable quantity. Step four
tests the schedulable quantity against the financial plan and escalates any
shortfall to system operations.

## 5. Approvals

The regional scheduling managers approve their own declarations. System
operations approves the consolidated schedule. No station may generate against
an undeclared schedule.

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions are handled through the shift despatch call. An exception raised on
the shift call does not amend the schedule; it authorises a departure from it,
which is reconciled in the energy accounts.
