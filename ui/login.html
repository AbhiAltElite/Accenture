Every movement, explained and signed.

When a business metric moves, WhyChain finds what caused it, tests every cause before it is shown, and hands the decision to the person accountable for it.

  • Every figure is computed by code. The language model writes prose and never a number.
  • Every cause passes timing, comparison and placebo tests before it is shown. When nothing passes, it says unknown.
  • Every sign-off and decision is kept on a tamper-evident audit trail.
